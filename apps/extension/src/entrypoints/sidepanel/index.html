Camera is off. Choose a mode, then start it when you want gesture control.
  • Point to move the page cursor.
  • Pinch and release to click; pinch and drag up or down to scroll.
  • Risky actions such as sending, buying, deleting, or submitting are blocked.
  • Focus a text field on the page before typing.
  • Point at a key and pinch to type. Shift and backspace are supported.
  • Air typing never presses Enter or submits a form.

Works on normal http/https pages. Camera video stays on this device and is stopped when you stop or close the panel.
