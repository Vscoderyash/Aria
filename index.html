JARVIS for your browser

The extension adds JARVIS to every tab. Talk to it, and it clicks, types, scrolls, opens sites and switches tabs for you. It plans bigger tasks, checks its own steps, and learns the shortcuts and corrections you teach it.

Download for Chrome, Edge or BraveDownload the desktop app

Install the extension

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Click the JARVIS icon, enter your site's address, and press Test.

The desktop app

Runs on your computer, listens anywhere (not only in the browser) and speaks in a natural male voice. It opens apps, sets timers, takes screenshots and controls volume and media. Keep it running and the extension uses it too.
